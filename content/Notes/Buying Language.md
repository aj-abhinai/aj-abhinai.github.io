Concept in marketing that tell you **how customers naturally describe their problem** and what they want 

Buying language has for parts 
- Current pain
- Emotional Frustration
- Failed attempts 
- Desired outcome 


How to find Buying language 

- Looks for places where people speak or share there information honestly - can be DM , YT , Reddit , Communities and how they describe it .

It is also interchangeably used as 
 - Voice of customer
 - Customer Language 

How to use 
- Start collecting and documenting customer language instead of writing founder copy
- use customer wording instead of business jargon
- Learn objection instead of Guessing 
- Build headings from rather than inventing
